Case Studies

Case Study 01

Unshareable Stories

BGC Canada · Copywriter, Creative Lead, Broadcast Script Writer

BGC Canada needed Canadians to feel the cost of growing up without support. Not through statistics. Not through charity speak. Through the specific, quiet moments that kids carry alone because there's nowhere safe to share them.

"Kids shouldn't have unshareable stories."

Simple. The weight comes from what it doesn't say. The line works across every issue BGC touches because the structure never changes — only the story does. Wrote and shaped alumni narratives for the campaign hub, TVC scripts with direction notes, and social copy that introduced real kids by name, one story at a time, with no call to action. Just the story and the line.

The brief could have produced a campaign that made people feel guilty. It didn't. It made them feel something more useful: recognition. Every adult has been one of these kids, or knows one. The copy never told audiences what to think. It put them inside a specific moment, with a specific child, and trusted them to arrive at the feeling themselves.

Case Study 02

Mattamy Homes

The Turn Lab · Copywriter

A home is the biggest purchase most people ever make. The brief was deceptively simple: write the website, the community pages, the social campaigns. The real challenge was harder. The copy couldn't just describe square footage and highway access. It had to make someone feel like they already belonged there before they ever set foot inside.

Every word had to work across buyer types, price points, and communities with completely different characters, while sounding like it came from one voice. Not a builder's voice. A buyer's voice. Real estate copy fails when it describes. It succeeds when it imagines.
