Case Study 01

BGC Canada

Copywriter · Creative Lead · Broadcast Script Writer
TVC · Social · OOH · Campaign Hub · Alumni Narratives

Unshareable
Stories

The Brief & The Idea

BGC Canada needed Canadians to feel the cost of growing up without support. Not through statistics. Not through charity speak. Through the specific, quiet moments that kids carry alone because there's nowhere safe to share them.

"Kids shouldn't have unshareable stories."

Simple. The weight comes from what it doesn't say. The line needed to work across every issue BGC touches. It does because the structure never changes, only the story does. Wrote and shaped alumni narratives for the campaign hub, TVC scripts with full direction notes, and social copy that introduced real kids by name. No call to action. Just the story and the line. The restraint is the craft.

Campaign Hub ↗ Bullying TVC ↗ Hunger TVC ↗ Full Script + Direction Notes ↗
Why It Worked

The brief could have produced a campaign that made people feel guilty. It didn't. It made them feel something more useful: recognition. Every adult has been one of these kids, or knows one.

The copy never told audiences what to think. It put them inside a specific moment, with a specific child, and trusted them to arrive at the feeling themselves. That's the difference between advertising that runs and advertising that stays with people.

Case Study 02

Mattamy Homes · The Turn Lab

Copywriter
Corporate Website · Community Pages · Design Studio · Social · Sales Collateral

Make Them
Feel It First

The Brief & The Idea

A home is the biggest purchase most people ever make. The brief was deceptively simple: write the website, the community pages, the social campaigns. The real challenge was harder. The copy couldn't describe square footage and highway access. It had to make someone feel like they already belonged there before they ever set foot inside.

"Not a builder's voice. A buyer's voice."

Every word had to work across buyer types, price points, and communities with completely different characters, while sounding like it came from one voice. Every community had its own narrative. One brand voice throughout.

Corporate Site ↗ Community Page ↗ Design Studio ↗ Social Post ↗
Why It Worked

Real estate copy fails when it describes. It succeeds when it imagines. The brief required writing that could make someone picture Sunday morning coffee on a back porch in a community that didn't exist yet.

The copy that performed best was the copy that trusted the reader, gave them just enough to see themselves there, and let their imagination do the rest.

Case Study 03

BGC Canada

Copywriter · Campaign Lead
Event Copy · Social · Press · Partner Coordination

BGC
Club Day

The Brief & The Idea

BGC Canada wanted to create a national day of celebration for its 600+ Clubs. A feel-good charity day wasn't enough. It needed to feel like an event people genuinely wanted to be part of: not something they were asked to support.

"Turn a national nonprofit into a national celebration."

Bring in partners with genuine cultural reach. Light up the country in BGC green. Give people a reason to show up, share, and pay attention. Make BGC impossible to ignore for one day.

Media Release ↗ Annual Report 2023 ↗
Why It Worked

The brief asked for awareness. The campaign delivered credibility. Walk Off the Earth performed live on BGC's Facebook. CityTV and CBC Evening News covered Jully Black's visit to BGC East Scarborough. 41 civic activations. 25 landmarks lit in BGC green, including the CN Tower, Niagara Falls, the Montreal Olympic Stadium.

Earned media, organic celebrity amplification, civic buy-in from 11 city halls. These aren't things you buy. They're things you earn when the creative and the cause are strong enough that people want to be associated with them.
